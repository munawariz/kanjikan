One-line: The Atlas debit/credit card artwork, used in heroes, feature cards and the app's wallet.

```jsx
<BankCard width={360} />
<BankCard finish="sage" network="Mastercard" width={280} />
```

Notes
- Finishes: forest (flagship), sage (secondary), cream (credit). Nothing else.
- The two cropped rings are a brand shape, not a logo — keep them bleeding off the card edge.
- Cards always sit at a slight rotation in marketing layouts (`transform: rotate(-8deg)`) and flat in the app.
