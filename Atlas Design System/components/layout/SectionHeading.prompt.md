One-line: Standard section opener — uppercase eyebrow, tight display headline, optional grey paragraph.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Our features" title={<>4 Quick Steps To Use Our<br/>Atlas Services</>} align="center" />
<SectionHeading title="Meet Money Without Borders" action={<Button>Send Money Now</Button>} />
```

Notes
- Headline always `--tracking-display` (-0.03em) and `--leading-display` (1.05). Break lines manually with `<br/>` for rhythm.
- Eyebrow is grey on light, lime on forest.
